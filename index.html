OwnX Progress

Everything we've shipped, and what's next.

OwnX builds AI you control. Own1 is a personal AI computer for your home or office. Nexy is its assistant, on your laptop, your phone and in the cloud too. OwnX Console runs AI apps on servers you choose, and Earth Compute maps what that computing power costs around the world.

What's new

  1. Nov 2026Nexy apps
    Your phone, powered by your gaming PC

    A phone with no AI model of its own asked for an image. The Own1 passed the job to a gaming PC's graphics card and returned it in 17 seconds, about five times faster than the Own1 on its own. Every result says which device made it.

  2. Nov 2026Nexy apps
    Nexy beta for Mac, Windows and Android

    One download page with a step-by-step install guide. All three apps tell you when an update is ready.

  3. Nov 2026Own1
    Add a device with an 8-character code

    Your Own1 shows a short code, you type it into Nexy, and you approve the device on the Own1 itself. Guest codes let family or a team use your Own1 within limits you set.

  4. Nov 2026Security
    Independent security reviews before the beta

    Two reviews of the apps, the web app, the agent engine and the Own1, before opening to testers. The issues found in the apps are fixed.

  5. Nov 2026Nexy Engine
    A new AI agent engine, built small

    Written in Rust, it is one of the smallest agent engines around: a single 3.5 MB program that leaves the machine's memory to the AI model. Open source.

  6. Nov 2026Private network
    Open your Own1 from anywhere

    The apps reach your Own1 over an encrypted network and sign in with a key only you hold. No router changes, no third-party cloud in the middle.
