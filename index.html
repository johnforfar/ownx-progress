OwnX Progress

Everything we've shipped, and what's next.

OwnX builds AI you control. Own1 is a personal AI computer for your home or office. Nexy is its assistant, on your laptop, your phone and in the cloud too. OwnX Console runs AI apps on servers you choose, and Earth Compute maps what that computing power costs around the world.

This page tracks engineering progress. Much of it is in beta or early access, may not be available on every device, and can change before general release. A feature is released when it is announced as released.
